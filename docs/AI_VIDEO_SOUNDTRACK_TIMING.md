# Offline soundtrack production

The main local editor now supports an initial source trim, movie entrance, scheduled playback duration, independent fades, volume, loop toggle and silence outside the selected segment. Integer milliseconds are bounded and validated against the native 16/24 FPS timeline. A duration of zero fills the remaining movie. Non-looping segments must fit the real original audio duration; looping repeats the original track after its first trim. The source preview is unedited. All settings persist in metadata-only drafts and the storyboard, are checked in the portable launcher, and are compared against the render receipt. MP4 and WebM retain the rendered audio. Older projects keep their existing loop behavior. This is one soundtrack track, not a multi-track mixer. No external AI, upload or GPU.
