# Offline cinematic camera motion

Choose static framing, push in, pull out, pan left/right or tilt up/down, at 10, 20 or 30 percent zoom strength. Each shot gets its own cubic easing from first to final frame, using FFmpeg zoompan with one output frame per input and explicit 16/24 FPS. Motion follows composition and grading, before fades and held-frame scene transitions. It is digital cropping of the composed frame, including bars if contain framing is selected; not a physical camera reconstruction or AI generation. The project-wide preset is saved in the board and draft, and verified by the launcher and receipt. Original source preview remains unchanged. Old projects omit camera settings. Regression tests check actual decoded marker position and size for all six moves, with exact original frame count. No external service, upload or GPU.
