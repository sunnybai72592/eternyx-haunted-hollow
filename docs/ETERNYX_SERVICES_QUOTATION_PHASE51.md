# Services Phase 51 — commercial quotation and acceptance foundation

## What is implemented
The exact staging review candidate scripts/sql/services-staging-quotation-agreements-candidate.sql introduces two RLS-protected tables, a security-invoker quotation register and two invoker write guards. Administrators publish immutable USD quotations against a persisted scope review and a request at proposal_sent. Amounts are integer cents, bounded to 1–100000000, with server-assigned revision/time and 5-minute to 90-day validity. One owner may explicitly acknowledge the latest unexpired quotation. Prior revisions remain readable by participants; superseded/expired/closed/accepted state is derived from persisted facts. A unique acceptance and shared per-request advisory lock prevent conflicting quote writes; guards require READ COMMITTED isolation for fresh snapshots after lock acquisition. The commercial record is not a signature, payment, legal agreement or fulfillment authorization.

## Security and acceptance
Customers cannot publish quotes, impersonate another owner, accept obsolete/expired/unacknowledged terms, overwrite a price, erase acceptance, or inspect unrelated requests. Admins cannot accept on behalf of a customer or replace a quote after acceptance. Anon, service_role and the restricted auditor are not granted application access. Existing delivery status guard is not changed. Rehearsal applies the exact candidate after the original 13-file chain, hardened administrator helper and Phase50 review journey in isolated PG17, then runs actual SQL role tests with rollback. Separate real concurrent publication/acceptance and HTTP/JWT acceptance remain required before launch. The request status itself is not serialized by the quote lock: quotation acceptance alone is never permission to start work.

## Boundaries and remaining work
USD-only and the amount/expiry bounds are provisional design defaults. Existing request deletion/account erasure behavior needs explicit commercial retention and deletion review (restrict foreign keys deliberately protect accepted quote/scope evidence); no automatic deletion or migration of existing records. The candidate is intentionally outside the pinned auto-migration chain: no live deployment is approved by merging. A new deployment requires review of exact SQL, live compatibility and grants. Customer/admin quotation UI, dedicated real staging accounts, browser and concurrency acceptance, legal terms approval, order fulfillment, payment integration and recovery are not yet delivered. Never equate quote acceptance to a completed order.
