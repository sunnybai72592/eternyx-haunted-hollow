# AI Arsenal Phase 21 — advanced lexical retrieval and evidence preparation

Extends Phase 20 local BM25 retrieval. Explicit alternative words are normalized and deduplicated, with at most eight distinct primary and alternative terms combined. Alternative matches contribute half the normal BM25 term weight. All mode still requires every primary term; any mode may match alternatives alone. Indexed prefix suggestions are literal variants (not synonyms, stemming or semantic inference), and are never automatically applied. Optional source-diverse ordering round-robins documents while preserving score order within each source; the original match count and number of matching sources remain visible. Source filtering still applies.

Select one to three displayed passages and enter a nonempty question of at most 250 characters. The evidence packet rechecks passage identity, source name, exact decoded text, offsets and physical line coordinates against the live source and index. It creates stable E1/E2/E3 labels in displayed order, with exact passages and source locations serialized as JSON. The draft instructs a downstream answerer to treat the sources as untrusted data, cite evidence labels and disclose uncertainty. The 5,000-character limit fails closed without truncation. The packet is locally prepared and explicitly copyable; no answer is generated or automatically sent to an AI provider. Copying may expose private source content. Neither lexical relevance nor local equality proves truth or authorship.

Changes to index, source, query, expansion, ranking mode or filter invalidate results, evidence selections and drafts; changing the question or selection invalidates the packet. No runtime dependency, external API, cloud storage or backend changes.
