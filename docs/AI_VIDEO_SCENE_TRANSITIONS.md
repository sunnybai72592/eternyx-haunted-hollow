# Frame-preserving cinematic transitions

The main editor supports hard cuts, held-frame dissolve and four directional wipes. Select 250 or 500 ms (4/8 frames at 16 FPS and 6/12 at 24 FPS). Each incoming scene must be longer than that window; a transition requires at least two scenes. The previous composed shot's last decoded frame is blended with the incoming scene, not a conventional overlapping crossfade: no source cut, overall duration, caption cue, audio placement or frame count changes. FFmpeg applies the effect after color, framing and per-shot fades; existing fade-out to black will influence the held frame. The preset is project-wide, not per-cut. The source preview remains original-only. Old drafts and boards omit the optional transition and keep hard cuts. The board SHA-256 and receipt bind the chosen preset. Tests decode real frames from both timebases, check all five transition effects, original scene boundaries and exact caption timing. No upload, GPU or remote API.
