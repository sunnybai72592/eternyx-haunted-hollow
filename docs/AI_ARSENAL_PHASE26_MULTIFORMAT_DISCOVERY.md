# AI Arsenal Phase 26 — multiformat discovery

Extends the bounded local Office ZIP/XML parser to standard PPTX presentation text. Resolves slide relationships through the actual presentation order rather than numeric filenames, permits at most 60 slides, rejects duplicate or missing slide targets and external slide relationships, and preserves slide numbers when textless slides are skipped. DrawingML text runs, fields and line breaks in the slide shape tree are extracted; hidden slides are labeled. Speaker notes, images, charts, visual reading order, animations, embedded objects and layout are not rendered or interpreted. Existing archive, extracted-text and corpus limits remain. Selected slides are imported as separate TXT sources with original slide-order labels. No new runtime dependency or upload.

Local discovery explicitly processes current corpus text: declared format, decoded character and line counts, Markdown-style headings, cell-reference-like lines, eligible term frequencies and bounded previews of literal key/value lines with line numbers. This is descriptive profiling, not trained document classification. The relationship map calculates TF-IDF cosine similarity over normalized Unicode words (minimum three characters, limited English stop-word list). It reports pairs with at least one eligible shared word or exact decoded-text equality. Exact text equality is separate from lexical overlap; score is not a probability, authorship test, semantic relationship or evidence agreement. Shared term buttons perform real indexed searches across the current collection and invalidate previous evidence selections. Counts-only reports omit source names, terms and field values.

Discovery is in browser memory; source changes release the report and hide stale results. Local Office extraction is not a formal security audit, and actual browser compatibility remains a release gate. Existing optional AI synthesis still requires its own review and consent. No new paid service, provider, backend change or deployment.
